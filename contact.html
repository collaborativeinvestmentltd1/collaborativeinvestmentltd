Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How soon can CIL begin managing a business?

Assessment can begin within 1–2 weeks. Full management typically starts in 4–6 weeks after thorough due diligence and planning.

Do you work with diaspora clients?

Yes. We specialize in business & asset management for Nigerians abroad, with full transparency, monthly reporting, and secure communication channels.

What is the minimum investment?

Entry-level investments begin at ₦5,000,000 depending on the venture. Custom partnerships and smaller investments are also available for specific projects.

How often do investors receive reports?

Monthly financial reports + quarterly performance reviews + annual comprehensive audits. Diaspora clients receive additional video conference updates.

Do you operate outside Lagos and Ogun?

Yes. We are expanding and can manage businesses nationwide depending on project scope. Currently operating in Lagos, Ogun, and planning expansion to Oyo State.

What's the typical response time?

Initial response within 2 hours during business hours. Comprehensive proposal within 24-48 hours for investment inquiries.
