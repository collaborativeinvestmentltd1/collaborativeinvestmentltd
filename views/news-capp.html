CIL Property Launches CAPP Program

October 2025 Company Update 5 min read

CIL Property Management has launched the CAPP (CIL Annual Preservation Program), a comprehensive property maintenance and upgrade service for property owners. The CAPP program is designed to protect and enhance property value through structured annual maintenance, inspections, and professional upgrades.

Property owners can now enroll their assets in a comprehensive preservation plan that ensures their properties remain in optimal condition year-round. This innovative program addresses a critical need in Nigeria's property market, where many properties suffer from neglect and deferred maintenance, leading to significant value depreciation.

The Challenge: Property Depreciation in Nigeria

In Nigeria, property owners face significant challenges in maintaining their assets. Poor maintenance, lack of professional oversight, and limited access to quality service providers often lead to property deterioration and value loss. This is particularly challenging for absentee owners, diaspora investors, and those with multiple properties.

The consequences of neglected property maintenance include:

  • Value Depreciation: Properties lose value due to poor maintenance
  • Tenant Turnover: Poorly maintained properties experience higher tenant turnover
  • Reduced Rental Income: Properties in poor condition command lower rents
  • Costly Repairs: Deferred maintenance leads to more expensive repairs
  • Safety Hazards: Poor maintenance creates safety risks for occupants

What CAPP Includes

The CAPP program offers a comprehensive suite of services designed to keep properties in optimal condition:

  • Annual Inspections: Comprehensive property assessments conducted twice a year by qualified professionals
  • Preventive Maintenance: Scheduled maintenance to identify and address issues before they become major problems
  • Repairs & Upgrades: Professional repairs and value-enhancing upgrades performed by certified contractors
  • Documentation: Complete records of all maintenance activities, inspections, and improvements
  • Digital Dashboard: Real-time tracking of property condition and maintenance history
  • Emergency Response: 24/7 emergency maintenance support
  • Seasonal Maintenance: Specialized maintenance for different seasons and weather conditions

Benefits for Property Owners

Property owners who enroll in the CAPP program enjoy numerous benefits:

  • Value Protection: Protects property value and prevents depreciation
  • Early Detection: Identifies issues before they become major problems
  • Tenant Satisfaction: Enhances property appeal and tenant satisfaction
  • Transparency: Complete transparency and documentation of all maintenance activities
  • Peace of Mind: Peace of mind knowing your property is professionally maintained
  • Cost Savings: Reduces long-term maintenance costs through preventive care
  • Professional Management: Access to professional property management expertise
  • Customized Plans: Tailored preservation plans for each property's unique needs

How the Program Works

Enrolling your property in the CAPP program is simple and straightforward:

  • Initial Assessment: Contact CIL Property Management for a comprehensive initial assessment of your property
  • Customized Plan: Receive a customized preservation plan tailored to your property's specific needs
  • Enrollment: Enroll in the program and start enjoying professional maintenance services
  • Ongoing Management: Regular inspections, maintenance, and upgrades as scheduled
  • Digital Access: Access your property's digital dashboard for real-time updates and documentation
  • Annual Review: Annual review of the preservation plan to ensure it continues to meet your needs

Who Should Enroll

The CAPP program is ideal for:

  • Property Investors: Investors with multiple properties needing professional maintenance
  • Absentee Owners: Owners who live away from their properties and need reliable management
  • Diaspora Investors: Nigerians abroad with property in Nigeria
  • Commercial Property Owners: Owners of commercial properties requiring specialized maintenance
  • Residential Landlords: Landlords wanting to maximize tenant satisfaction and rental income
  • Estate Managers: Managers of estates and large property portfolios

Pricing and Packages

The CAPP program offers flexible packages to suit different property types and budgets:

  • Bronze Package: Basic annual inspections and preventive maintenance
  • Silver Package: Comprehensive maintenance including repairs and upgrades
  • Gold Package: Full-service preservation with priority response and premium services
  • Platinum Package: Complete property management including tenant relations and financial reporting

Custom packages are also available for unique property requirements.

"The CAPP program represents a new standard in property preservation in Nigeria," said the Head of CIL Property Management. "We're giving property owners the tools and expertise to protect their most valuable assets. This program reflects our commitment to excellence in property management and our belief that every property deserves professional care."

Since its launch, the CAPP program has received strong interest from property owners across Nigeria, with over 50 properties already enrolled. The program has already demonstrated significant value, with participating properties showing improved tenant satisfaction, reduced maintenance costs, and enhanced property value.

To learn more about the CAPP program or to enroll your property, please contact our team or visit our Property page.
