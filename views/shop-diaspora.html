Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from diaspora investors answered clearly and transparently.

How do I invest from outside Nigeria? +

Our investment process is fully digital and designed for diaspora investors. You can:

  • Schedule a virtual consultation via video call
  • Sign all documents electronically using our secure platform
  • Transfer funds through our international banking partners
  • Monitor your investment through our online dashboard

We handle everything in Nigeria on your behalf.

How are my investments protected? +

We provide multiple layers of protection for your investment:

  • Asset Collateralization: All investments are backed by tangible assets
  • Legal Documentation: Registered investment agreements with Nigerian authorities
  • Insurance Coverage: Comprehensive insurance on all projects
  • Transparent Operations: Regular audits and financial reporting
  • Directorship Option: Option to join project boards for oversight
How do I receive my returns? +

Returns are distributed quarterly through the following methods:

  • International Bank Transfer: Direct to your foreign bank account
  • Digital Wallets: Transfer to your preferred digital wallet
  • Naira Account: Option to keep returns in Nigeria for reinvestment
  • Automatic Reinvestment: Option to automatically reinvest returns

All transfers include detailed statements and are fully documented.

What is the minimum investment amount? +

We offer flexible investment tiers to suit different investor profiles:

  • Starter Tier: ₦5,000,000 minimum (approx. $3,200)
  • Growth Tier: ₦15,000,000 minimum (approx. $9,600)
  • Premium Tier: ₦30,000,000 minimum (approx. $19,200)
  • VIP Tier: ₦50,000,000+ minimum (approx. $32,000+)

Larger investments receive preferential returns and additional benefits.

Can I visit my investment projects? +

Yes, we encourage investors to visit their projects. We offer:

  • Virtual Tours: Live video tours of projects from anywhere
  • Scheduled Visits: Organized visits when you're in Nigeria
  • Live Cameras: 24/7 access to project site cameras
  • Project Reports: Detailed photographic and video documentation
  • Manager Meetings: Direct access to project managers

We handle all logistics for investor visits to Nigeria.
