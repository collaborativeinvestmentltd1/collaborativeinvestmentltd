Our Complete Business Management Services

We manage operations across multiple integrated sectors. You retain full ownership while we handle daily management, reporting, and growth of your business.

1. Agribusiness Management

Poultry (layers/broilers), fish farming (catfish), piggery, and crop production. We manage the full value chain: feeding, veterinary care, biosecurity, and market sales. Our farms supply CIL hotels, guaranteeing offtake. Transparent daily records and cost control.

  • Day‑old chicks to market
  • Automated feeding & water
  • Veterinary protocols
  • Direct sales & reporting
2. Construction, Fabrication & Project Management

Block manufacturing (6", 9", hollow), steel fabrication (poultry cages, gates, basketball courts), and full construction project supervision. We eliminate material theft, supervise artisans, and deliver quality. Daily capacity 5,000+ blocks.

  • Automated block plants
  • Fabrication workshop
  • Project timelines & budgets
  • Material sourcing
3. Machinery & Industrial Services

Sales, rental, and maintenance of generators, farm machinery, construction equipment. We manage a rental pool with 95% uptime guarantee, backup deployment, and spare parts supply. Also importation and fabrication of custom machines.

  • Generator sales & service
  • Equipment rental (pumps, block machines)
  • Preventive maintenance contracts
  • Industrial fabrication
4. Solar Energy Solutions

Full solar business management: retail shops, installation, PPA/lease models, and maintenance. We run investor‑owned solar branches – you provide capital, we handle operations. Guaranteed orders from CIL properties. 24/7 monitoring.

  • Solar retail & installation
  • PPA & lease financing
  • Technical support & maintenance
  • Investor branch management
5. Furniture Production & Supply

Custom furniture manufacturing, leather upholstery, and showroom management. We run production workshops (investor‑owned or as a service). Supply to CIL hotels, offices, and external clients. Quality control and installation.

  • Office & home furniture
  • Leather upholstery
  • Workshop & artisan management
  • Catalog & custom orders
6. Hotel Turnaround Services

Transform underperforming hotels into profitable properties. We take over operations: front desk, housekeeping, kitchen, bar. Revenue management, staff training, and integration with CIL supply chain (furniture, solar, food).

  • Full operational takeover
  • Staff restructuring & training
  • Bar/menu styling & inventory
  • Online booking & marketing
7. Business Facility Management

One‑contract facility management for offices, hotels, and properties. Our in‑house team covers: annual repainting, solar maintenance, furniture installation, electrical/plumbing, and preventive maintenance. Integrated workforce.

  • Repainting & refurbishment
  • Solar system maintenance
  • Electrical & plumbing repairs
  • Preventive schedules
8. Property & Tenant Management

Complete management for property owners – especially diaspora investors. Tenant screening, rent collection, 24/7 maintenance, and quarterly 3D scans (for diaspora). We also facilitate upgrade financing (loan repaid from rental income).

  • Tenant sourcing & lease
  • Rent collection & remittance
  • 24/7 maintenance hotline
  • Asset preservation (CAPP)
9. Diaspora Asset Management

A single platform to manage all your Nigerian assets – property, farm, hotel, solar, furniture. Dedicated relationship officer, unified dashboard, quarterly 3D scans, and automated remittance. You invest, we manage, you monitor from anywhere.

  • Single dashboard
  • Quarterly 3D property scans
  • Automated rent remittance
  • Dedicated officer
10. Find Work / Hire Professional Workers (CIL Workforce)

A CIL job platform connecting skilled workers with businesses. cilworkforce.com – post jobs, find verified technicians, artisans, and professionals. Integrated with CIL sectors, so we know the talent pool.

  • For job seekers: find work
  • For businesses: hire vetted staff
  • Daily job postings
  • Direct applications
