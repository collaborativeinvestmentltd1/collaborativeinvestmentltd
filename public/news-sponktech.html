Strategic Partnership with Sponktech Energy

April 2026 Partnership 5 min read

Collaborative Investment Ltd (CIL) is proud to announce a strategic partnership with Sponktech Energy Limited, a leading renewable energy solutions provider. This collaboration marks a significant milestone in CIL's mission to deliver comprehensive, sustainable energy solutions across Nigeria.

By combining Sponktech Energy's technical expertise with CIL's integrated business ecosystem, the partnership aims to provide reliable, affordable solar energy to homes and businesses nationwide. This strategic alliance will accelerate the adoption of renewable energy in Nigeria, contributing to the country's sustainable development goals and energy transition agenda.

About Sponktech Energy Limited

Sponktech Energy Limited is a leading renewable energy company specializing in solar power solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial applications. With years of experience in the Nigerian market, Sponktech has built a reputation for quality, reliability, and innovation in the renewable energy sector.

The company's expertise spans solar panel installation, system design, maintenance, and after-sales support, making them an ideal partner for CIL's solar energy division. Their commitment to quality and customer satisfaction aligns perfectly with CIL's values of transparency, professionalism, and excellence.

What This Partnership Means

The partnership with Sponktech Energy Limited enhances CIL's solar energy offerings in several key ways:

  • Premium Equipment Access: CIL gains access to top-tier solar equipment and technology from leading manufacturers, ensuring high-quality installations.
  • Expert Installation: Sponktech's certified technicians bring years of installation experience, ensuring optimal system performance.
  • Competitive Pricing: Through bulk procurement and strategic partnerships, customers benefit from competitive pricing on solar systems.
  • Technical Support: Enhanced technical support and training for CIL's solar team ensures continuous improvement and customer satisfaction.
  • Expanded Reach: The partnership enables CIL to expand its solar services across Nigeria, reaching more communities and businesses.

Impact on Nigeria's Renewable Energy Landscape

This partnership comes at a critical time when Nigeria is actively seeking to diversify its energy mix and reduce reliance on fossil fuels. With the country facing significant energy challenges, including unreliable grid power and high electricity costs, solar energy presents a viable and sustainable alternative.

Through this collaboration, CIL and Sponktech Energy will contribute to:

  • Energy Access: Providing reliable, affordable solar energy to underserved communities
  • Job Creation: Creating employment opportunities in the renewable energy sector
  • Skills Development: Training and certifying local technicians in solar installation and maintenance
  • Environmental Impact: Reducing carbon emissions and promoting sustainable energy practices

What's Next

Through this partnership, CIL and Sponktech Energy will work together to:

  • Deploy solar systems across CIL-managed properties, including hotels, residential estates, and commercial facilities
  • Offer PPA (Power Purchase Agreement) financing options to make solar more accessible
  • Train and certify solar installation technicians through the CIL Workforce Academy
  • Provide ongoing maintenance and support for all solar installations
  • Expand solar access to underserved communities through targeted initiatives
  • Develop innovative solar solutions for agribusiness and industrial applications

"This partnership with Sponktech Energy represents our commitment to providing sustainable, affordable energy solutions to Nigerians," said the Managing Director of Collaborative Investment Ltd. "Together, we will power homes, businesses, and communities across the country, contributing to Nigeria's sustainable development and energy security."

The partnership is already yielding results, with several projects in the pipeline, including the installation of solar systems at CIL-managed hotels and commercial properties. These projects will serve as showcases for the potential of solar energy in Nigeria, demonstrating its viability and benefits to a wider audience.
