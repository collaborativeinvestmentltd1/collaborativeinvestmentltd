CIL Workforce Academy Launches

November 2025 Program Launch 6 min read

Collaborative Investment Ltd (CIL) is proud to announce the launch of the CIL Workforce Academy, a comprehensive skills training and certification program designed to address Nigeria's skills gap. The Academy represents a significant investment in Nigeria's human capital development, offering practical, job-ready skills training across multiple sectors.

The program is designed to equip participants with the skills needed to succeed in today's competitive job market, while also creating a pipeline of qualified workers for CIL's growing ecosystem. With Nigeria facing a persistent skills gap that limits economic growth and employment opportunities, the CIL Workforce Academy aims to bridge this divide through targeted, industry-relevant training.

The Skills Gap Challenge in Nigeria

Nigeria faces a significant skills gap that undermines economic growth and employment opportunities. Despite having a large and youthful population, many young Nigerians lack the practical skills needed for employment. This gap exists across multiple sectors, including construction, renewable energy, agribusiness, and facility management.

The consequences of this skills gap are far-reaching:

  • High Unemployment: Many young people remain unemployed due to lack of relevant skills
  • Underemployment: Workers are often employed in roles that don't match their skills
  • Low Productivity: Businesses struggle with low productivity due to skill shortages
  • Reduced Competitiveness: Nigeria's economy becomes less competitive globally
  • Limited Economic Growth: The skills gap constrains economic growth and development

Program Overview

The CIL Workforce Academy offers comprehensive training in several key sectors:

  • Construction Skills: Building techniques, project management, safety protocols, equipment operation, and quality control
  • Solar Installation: Panel installation, system design, maintenance, troubleshooting, and safety procedures
  • Agribusiness: Farm management, poultry farming, aquaculture, crop production, and sustainable agriculture
  • Facility Management: Maintenance, cleaning, security, HVAC systems, and facility operations
  • Furniture Production: Design, manufacturing, upholstery, quality control, and supply chain management
  • Property Management: Tenant relations, property maintenance, administration, and asset management

Program Structure

The CIL Workforce Academy is structured to provide participants with both theoretical knowledge and practical experience:

  • Classroom Training: Comprehensive theoretical instruction from industry experts
  • Hands-on Practice: Practical training in real-world settings, including CIL's facilities and partner organizations
  • Internships: Opportunities to gain practical experience through internships with CIL and partner companies
  • Mentorship: Dedicated mentorship from experienced professionals
  • Certification: Industry-recognized certification upon program completion
  • Job Placement: Support with job placement, including connections to CIL's network of employers

Why This Matters

The CIL Workforce Academy addresses Nigeria's skills gap by providing:

  • Hands-on Training: Practical, job-ready skills training from industry experts
  • Industry Recognition: Certification that is recognized by employers across various sectors
  • Job Placement Support: Connections to employers and job placement assistance
  • Ecosystem Access: Access to the CIL ecosystem for employment and business opportunities
  • Entrepreneurship Support: Support for graduates who want to start their own businesses
  • Continuous Learning: Opportunities for ongoing skills development and upskilling

Who Can Apply

The program is open to a diverse range of participants:

  • Recent Graduates: Young people seeking practical skills to complement their education
  • Career Changers: Professionals looking to transition into new sectors
  • Entrepreneurs: Business owners looking to build skills in key sectors
  • Youth: Young people seeking employment opportunities
  • Women: Women looking to enter technical fields and build careers
  • Community Members: Individuals from communities where CIL operates

Program Impact and Success Stories

Since its soft launch, the CIL Workforce Academy has already made a significant impact:

  • 100+ Graduates: Over 100 participants have graduated from the program
  • 80% Placement Rate: 80% of graduates have secured employment or started businesses
  • 50+ Employers: Partnered with over 50 employers across various sectors
  • 15+ Training Locations: Training delivered across 15 locations in Nigeria
  • Business Creation: 20+ businesses created by graduates

"The CIL Workforce Academy is our commitment to building Nigeria's workforce of the future," said the Chief Operations Officer at CIL. "We're not just training people — we're creating pathways to meaningful employment and economic independence. The Academy represents our belief that with the right skills, every Nigerian can build a better future."

Future plans for the Academy include expanding to more locations, introducing new training programs, and developing partnerships with more employers and educational institutions. The goal is to train 1,000+ participants by 2027, contributing significantly to Nigeria's workforce development.

To learn more about the CIL Workforce Academy or to apply, please contact our team or visit our Workforce page.
