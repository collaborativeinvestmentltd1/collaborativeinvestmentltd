CIL Certified Branch Expands

March 2025 Company Update 3 min read

Collaborative Investment Ltd (CIL) is excited to announce the expansion of our CIL Certified Branch model to 5 new locations across Nigeria.

This expansion brings our total branch network to 12 locations, with ambitious plans to reach 150+ branches by Year 5. Each branch is locally owned and operated, creating jobs and economic opportunities in their communities.

New Branch Locations

  • Abuja: Federal Capital Territory
  • Port Harcourt: Rivers State
  • Ibadan: Oyo State
  • Enugu: Enugu State
  • Kano: Kano State

About the Certified Branch Model

The CIL Certified Branch model enables entrepreneurs to operate CIL-certified businesses across the country. Each branch receives:

  • 6-week intensive training at the CIL Operations Academy
  • Access to CIL's technology platform and SOPs
  • Centralized procurement and supply chain support
  • Marketing and brand support
  • Regular audits and quality assurance

Economic Impact

Each CIL Certified Branch creates significant local economic impact:

  • 20-35 jobs created per branch
  • Local economic development and investment
  • Access to CIL's integrated ecosystem
  • Skills development and training opportunities
  • Community development and support

What's Next

CIL plans to continue its nationwide expansion, with the goal of establishing 150+ certified branches by Year 5. This ambitious growth plan will create thousands of jobs and bring CIL's integrated ecosystem to communities across Nigeria.

"The expansion of our certified branch network is a testament to the strength of our model," said the Managing Director of CIL. "We're building a national ecosystem that creates opportunities for entrepreneurs and communities alike."

To learn more about becoming a CIL Certified Branch partner, please contact our team or visit our Partners page.
