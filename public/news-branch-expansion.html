CIL Certified Branch Expands

March 2025 Company Update 5 min read

Collaborative Investment Ltd (CIL) is excited to announce the expansion of our CIL Certified Branch model to 5 new locations across Nigeria. This expansion brings our total branch network to 12 locations, with ambitious plans to reach 150+ branches by Year 5.

Each branch is locally owned and operated, creating jobs and economic opportunities in their communities. This expansion represents a significant milestone in CIL's mission to build a national ecosystem that creates opportunities for entrepreneurs and communities across Nigeria.

The Challenge: Economic Development in Nigeria

Nigeria faces significant challenges in economic development, including limited access to business opportunities, lack of entrepreneurial support, and restricted access to markets. Many communities lack access to the resources, training, and support needed to build successful businesses.

Key challenges include:

  • Limited Access: Limited access to business opportunities and markets
  • Entrepreneurial Support: Lack of training and support for entrepreneurs
  • Capital Constraints: Limited access to capital for business development
  • Market Access: Difficulty accessing markets and customers
  • Skills Gap: Limited skills and training opportunities
  • Infrastructure: Limited infrastructure and support systems

New Branch Locations

The expansion includes five strategically selected locations across Nigeria:

  • Abuja: Federal Capital Territory, providing access to the nation's capital and its surrounding communities
  • Port Harcourt: Rivers State, a key economic hub in the South-South region
  • Ibadan: Oyo State, the largest city in the South-West region
  • Enugu: Enugu State, a major economic center in the South-East region
  • Kano: Kano State, the largest city in the North and a major economic hub

About the Certified Branch Model

The CIL Certified Branch model is a unique franchise-like approach that enables entrepreneurs to operate CIL-certified businesses across the country. Each branch receives comprehensive support:

  • Training: 6-week intensive training at the CIL Operations Academy, covering all aspects of business operations
  • Technology: Access to CIL's technology platform and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
  • Supply Chain: Centralized procurement and supply chain support, ensuring quality and consistency
  • Marketing: Marketing and brand support, leveraging the CIL brand and reputation
  • Quality Assurance: Regular audits and quality assurance to maintain standards
  • Ongoing Support: Ongoing support from CIL headquarters for operational excellence
  • Networking: Access to CIL's network of partners and clients

Economic Impact

Each CIL Certified Branch creates significant local economic impact:

  • Job Creation: 20-35 direct jobs created per branch, with indirect employment through supply chains
  • Local Investment: Local economic development and investment, with branches sourcing goods and services locally
  • Ecosystem Access: Access to CIL's integrated ecosystem, providing diverse business opportunities
  • Skills Development: Skills development and training opportunities for local residents
  • Community Development: Community development and support through local engagement
  • Economic Multiplier: Significant economic multiplier effects through local spending and investment

Success Stories

The CIL Certified Branch model has already demonstrated success in existing locations:

  • Lagos Branch: Created 45 jobs and generated over ₦50 million in revenue in its first year
  • Ibadan Branch: Provided training to 200+ local entrepreneurs and community members
  • Port Harcourt Branch: Successfully launched multiple community development initiatives
  • Kano Branch: Established partnerships with local businesses and organizations
  • Enugu Branch: Created opportunities for youth employment and skills development

What's Next

CIL plans to continue its nationwide expansion with ambitious goals:

  • Short-Term (2025): Expand to 20 branches across Nigeria
  • Medium-Term (2026): Reach 50 branches, covering all geopolitical zones
  • Long-Term (2027): Establish 150+ certified branches nationwide
  • Regional Expansion: Explore opportunities for expansion into other African countries
  • Innovation: Continuously innovate the model to enhance impact and sustainability
  • Partnerships: Develop partnerships with government agencies and development organizations

"The expansion of our certified branch network is a testament to the strength of our model," said the Managing Director of CIL. "We're building a national ecosystem that creates opportunities for entrepreneurs and communities alike. This expansion represents our commitment to economic development and our belief in the potential of Nigerian entrepreneurs."

With the new branches now operational, CIL is already planning the next phase of expansion, with applications being received from entrepreneurs across Nigeria interested in joining the CIL Certified Branch network.
