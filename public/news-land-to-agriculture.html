Launch of Land to Agriculture Program

July 2025 Program Launch 5 min read

Collaborative Investment Ltd (CIL) is excited to announce the launch of our Land to Agriculture Program, designed to help landowners transform idle land into productive agricultural assets. This innovative program addresses one of Nigeria's most pressing challenges — underutilized land.

By providing end-to-end farm management, from setup to harvest, CIL enables landowners to generate sustainable income from their property while contributing to Nigeria's food security. The program is part of CIL's broader commitment to agricultural development and economic transformation in Nigeria.

The Challenge: Nigeria's Underutilized Land

Nigeria has vast agricultural potential, yet a significant portion of arable land remains underutilized. According to the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), Nigeria has over 70 million hectares of agricultural land, but only about 40% is currently being cultivated. This underutilization represents a massive missed opportunity for economic growth, food security, and job creation.

Many landowners lack the capital, expertise, or time to develop their land for agriculture. The Land to Agriculture Program addresses this gap by providing professional farm management services, making it easy for landowners to benefit from their property without the hassle of day-to-day farm operations.

How the Program Works

The Land to Agriculture Program is designed to be accessible and hassle-free for landowners:

  • Land Assessment: CIL experts evaluate your land's potential for agriculture, including soil quality, water availability, and climate conditions
  • Farm Setup: We handle all setup including clearing, preparation, and planting, using modern agricultural techniques and quality inputs
  • Professional Management: Full farm management from planting to harvest, including pest control, irrigation, and fertilization
  • Guaranteed Offtake: CIL hotels and properties purchase the harvest, ensuring a guaranteed market for produce
  • Transparent Reporting: Real-time updates on farm performance through our digital platform
  • Profit Distribution: Regular profit distribution to landowners based on farm performance

Who Can Participate

The program is open to a wide range of participants:

  • Landowners: Individuals with idle or underutilized land looking to generate income
  • Absentee Landowners: Landowners who live away from their property and need professional management
  • Diaspora Nigerians: Nigerians living abroad with land in Nigeria who want to benefit from agricultural investment
  • Investors: Investors looking for agricultural opportunities with predictable returns
  • Cooperative Societies: Community groups looking to develop agricultural projects
  • Family Estates: Families looking to preserve and generate income from inherited land

Benefits of the Program

Participants in the Land to Agriculture Program enjoy numerous benefits:

  • Professional Management: Expert farm management by CIL's experienced agricultural team
  • Guaranteed Market: Reliable offtake through CIL's integrated ecosystem, including hotels, restaurants, and other properties
  • Complete Transparency: Digital monitoring and regular reporting on farm performance
  • Competitive Returns: Attractive returns on land investment through agricultural production
  • Food Security Contribution: Help build a more food-secure Nigeria
  • Job Creation: Create employment opportunities in rural communities
  • Environmental Benefits: Sustainable farming practices that protect the environment

Success Stories

Since its soft launch, the Land to Agriculture Program has already seen promising results. Several landowners across Ogun, Oyo, and Kaduna states have enrolled in the program, with farms now producing poultry, fish, and various crops. The program has created over 50 jobs in rural communities and contributed to local economic development.

"The Land to Agriculture Program is a game-changer for landowners across Nigeria," said the Chief Operations Officer at CIL. "We're turning idle land into wealth-creating assets while building a more food-secure Nigeria. This program demonstrates our commitment to agricultural development and economic transformation."

Future Plans

CIL plans to expand the Land to Agriculture Program to more states across Nigeria, targeting areas with high agricultural potential. Future plans include:

  • Expanding to 10+ states by 2027
  • Increasing program capacity to 10,000+ hectares
  • Developing specialized programs for different agricultural sectors
  • Partnering with financial institutions for agricultural financing
  • Building a network of agricultural experts and trainers
