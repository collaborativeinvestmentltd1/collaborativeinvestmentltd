Diaspora Asset Management Console Now Live

February 2026 Company Update 5 min read

CIL Diaspora has launched its unified investment console, providing diaspora Nigerians with real‑time portfolio views across all CIL divisions. The platform is designed to address the unique needs of Nigerians living abroad who want to invest and manage assets in Nigeria.

With complete transparency, automated remittance, and dedicated relationship officers, the console makes it easier than ever for diaspora Nigerians to build wealth at home. This launch represents a significant milestone in CIL's commitment to serving the diaspora community and facilitating investment in Nigeria's economic development.

The Diaspora Opportunity in Nigeria

Nigeria's diaspora community represents a significant economic force. According to the World Bank, Nigeria receives over $20 billion in annual remittances, making it one of the largest recipients of diaspora remittances in Africa. Despite this substantial flow of funds, many diaspora Nigerians face challenges in effectively investing and managing assets in their home country.

Key challenges include:

  • Limited Information: Lack of reliable information about investment opportunities
  • Trust Issues: Concerns about the transparency and reliability of investments
  • Operational Blindness: Limited visibility into the performance of investments
  • Complex Processes: Complicated processes for investing and managing assets
  • Distance: Physical distance makes it difficult to monitor investments
  • Currency Volatility: Concerns about currency fluctuations and repatriation

Key Features

The CIL Diaspora console offers a comprehensive set of features designed to address the unique needs of diaspora investors:

  • Unified Dashboard: View all your investments in one place, with a clear overview of performance and value
  • Real‑Time Portfolio Views: Track investment performance across all CIL divisions in real-time
  • Complete Transparency: Full visibility into all transactions, performance metrics, and portfolio composition
  • Automated Remittance: Seamless funds transfer and repatriation with competitive exchange rates
  • Dedicated Relationship Officer: Personal support for every client, with a dedicated relationship officer to assist with all investment needs
  • Ecosystem Access: Invest in property, solar, agribusiness, and more through the integrated CIL ecosystem
  • Performance Reporting: Comprehensive performance reports with clear metrics and analysis
  • Secure Access: Bank-grade security with multi-factor authentication and encryption
