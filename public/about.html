About CIL

Collaborative Investment Ltd (CIL) is an integrated business development & investment management company operating across 12+ sectors in Nigeria.

Who We Are

Collaborative Investment Ltd (CIL) is Nigeria's first integrated business development and investment management platform. We start, manage, and scale businesses across multiple sectors — creating a self‑reinforcing ecosystem that builds jobs and sustainable value.

Our model solves the trust and operational deficit that plagues Nigeria's economy. By integrating 12+ divisions under one roof, we recapture value, guarantee performance, and formalize economic infrastructure.

RC Number: 8918603  |  Founded: 2024  |  Headquarters: Lagos, Nigeria

From agribusiness and construction to solar energy, property management, and workforce development — CIL is building the blueprint for Nigeria's economic transformation.
