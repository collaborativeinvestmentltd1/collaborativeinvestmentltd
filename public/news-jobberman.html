Partnership with JOBBERMAN NIGERIA

March 2026 Partnership 5 min read

Collaborative Investment Ltd (CIL) has entered into a strategic partnership with JOBBERMAN NIGERIA, one of the country's leading recruitment and HR platforms. This collaboration represents a significant step forward in CIL's mission to build Nigeria's workforce ecosystem.

By combining JOBBERMAN's extensive talent pool and recruitment technology with CIL's integrated business divisions, the partnership will create new employment opportunities across multiple sectors. This strategic alliance is expected to transform the recruitment landscape in Nigeria, making it easier for both employers and job seekers to find the perfect match.

The Challenge: Nigeria's Employment Gap

Nigeria faces a significant employment challenge, with millions of young people entering the workforce each year. Despite the availability of job opportunities, there is often a disconnect between employers and qualified candidates. This skills gap and mismatch has led to high unemployment rates, particularly among youth and recent graduates.

The partnership between CIL and JOBBERMAN NIGERIA directly addresses this challenge by creating a more efficient and effective recruitment ecosystem. By leveraging technology and expertise from both organizations, the partnership aims to connect more Nigerians with meaningful employment opportunities.

About JOBBERMAN NIGERIA

JOBBERMAN NIGERIA is a leading recruitment and HR platform connecting employers with qualified candidates. With a vast database of job seekers and innovative recruitment technology, JOBBERMAN has helped thousands of Nigerians find meaningful employment across various industries.

The platform offers a range of services including job posting, resume searching, candidate screening, and employer branding. JOBBERMAN's technology-driven approach ensures that employers find the right candidates quickly and efficiently, while job seekers have access to a wide range of opportunities.

What This Partnership Means

  • Access to Talent: CIL gains access to JOBBERMAN's extensive talent pool, with thousands of qualified candidates across various sectors
  • Employment Access: JOBBERMAN gains exclusive access to CIL's employment opportunities, providing their users with unique job openings
  • Joint Recruitment Drives: Collaborative recruitment drives across Nigeria, reaching more communities and candidates
  • Enhanced Technology: Improved job matching technology that better connects employers with qualified candidates
  • Skills Development: Joint skills development and placement programs to address the skills gap
  • Data Insights: Shared data and insights to better understand the Nigerian job market

Opportunities for Job Seekers

Through this partnership, job seekers will have access to:

  • Diverse Career Opportunities: Positions across CIL's 12+ business divisions, including property, solar, construction, agribusiness, and more
  • Multiple Locations: Jobs across various locations in Nigeria, including Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, and Kano
  • Skills Training: Access to training and certification programs through the CIL Workforce Academy
  • Career Development: Ongoing career development support and guidance
  • Competitive Compensation: Competitive salaries and benefits packages
  • Growth Opportunities: Opportunities for career advancement within the CIL ecosystem

Benefits for Employers

Employers partnering with CIL and JOBBERMAN can expect:

  • Access to Qualified Candidates: A wider pool of pre-screened, qualified candidates
  • Efficient Recruitment: Faster and more efficient recruitment processes
  • Reduced Hiring Costs: Cost-effective recruitment solutions
  • Skills Assessment: Access to skills assessment and verification tools
  • Workforce Planning: Support for workforce planning and development

Impact on Nigeria's Workforce

This partnership is expected to have a significant impact on Nigeria's workforce development:

  • Job Creation: Creating thousands of new job opportunities across multiple sectors
  • Skills Development: Addressing the skills gap through training and certification programs
  • Youth Employment: Creating employment opportunities for young Nigerians entering the workforce
  • Economic Growth: Contributing to economic growth through increased employment and productivity
  • Formalization: Formalizing the recruitment process and creating more structured employment pathways

"Partnering with JOBBERMAN NIGERIA strengthens our workforce division significantly," said the Managing Director of CIL. "Together, we will connect talented Nigerians with meaningful employment opportunities across our growing ecosystem. This partnership is a testament to our commitment to building a more prosperous Nigeria through job creation and skills development."

The partnership has already begun to yield results, with several successful recruitment drives already conducted. Plans are in place to expand the partnership to cover more sectors and regions, reaching more Nigerians with employment opportunities.
